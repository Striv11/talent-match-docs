# Jack & Jill India — Product Build Blueprint

## Executive summary
This project is a founder- and product-focused AI recruiting platform modeled on the Jack & Jill pattern, adapted for the Indian market.

The product has two sides:
- Jack: AI career agent for candidates
- Jill: AI hiring agent for companies

The goal is to help candidates discover relevant opportunities without mass-applying, and help companies hire better-fit talent faster without drowning in inbound noise.

## Product thesis
Hiring is broken because:
- candidates get overwhelmed by generic job boards and applications
- companies get flooded with low-signal inbound and weak outbound sourcing
- great candidates and good roles do not meet efficiently

We fix this with a two-sided matching platform:
- candidates share their career trajectory, preferences, and intent
- companies define the role and quality bar
- AI scores, ranks, and introduces the right opportunities
- humans stay in the loop for final decisions

## Market focus
Primary launch market:
- India
- Startup and growth-stage companies
- tech roles first: product, engineering, design, marketing, sales, operations

Primary city focus:
- Bengaluru
- Hyderabad
- Pune
- Delhi NCR
- Mumbai
- Chennai

## MVP success criteria
By launch, the product should support:
- candidate onboarding and profile creation
- role brief creation by companies
- AI-based candidate-role matching
- direct candidate-to-company introductions
- feedback-driven ranking
- basic pipeline tracking
- ATS-ready data structure
- success-fee revenue model

## Primary user stories
### Candidate stories
- I want to tell my story once and get relevant opportunities.
- I want to avoid spraying applications across dozens of roles.
- I want to see hidden or relevant jobs I would not have found alone.
- I want interview prep and salary guidance.

### Company stories
- I want fewer low-signal applicants and more relevant matches.
- I want faster access to strong passive and semi-passive candidates.
- I want to define the hiring brief and get ranked candidates quickly.
- I want a simple pipeline and direct introductions.

## Business model
- Candidates use the app free of charge.
- Companies pay a success fee only when a hire is successful.
- Recommended commercial range: 8%–12% of first-year CTC, adaptable by role type.

## Constraint
This is not a generic job board. It is a direct-introduction AI recruiting network built around trust, consent, matching quality, and feedback loops.
