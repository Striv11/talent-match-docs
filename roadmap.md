# MVP Roadmap and Execution Plan

## Phase 0 — Validation and positioning
Duration: 2–4 weeks

Goals:
- validate the Indian hiring pain points
- test startup and growth-stage demand
- confirm role focus and city focus

Tasks:
- identify target companies and role families
- run founder interviews and hiring manager discovery calls
- confirm compensation assumptions and role scope
- validate candidate pain points around noise and application fatigue

Outcome:
- initial GTM narrative and launch beta list

## Phase 1 — MVP build
Duration: 8–12 weeks

Goals:
- deliver end-to-end core hiring loop

Build list:
- candidate onboarding
- candidate profile creation
- role creation flow
- candidate matching service
- shortlist UI
- intro workflow
- company dashboard
- basic pipeline tracking
- notifications

Outcome:
- working MVP with a small set of companies and candidates

## Phase 2 — Trust and quality cycle
Duration: 4–6 weeks

Goals:
- improve matching quality and reduce low-signal intros

Tasks:
- recruiter feedback loop
- role-fit explanations
- ranking calibration
- candidate consent controls
- intro rate monitoring
- candidate quality review

Outcome:
- stronger match quality and trust layer

## Phase 3 — Productization
Duration: 6–8 weeks

Goals:
- turn the MVP into a dependable platform

Tasks:
- ATS integrations
- Slack workflow
- team permissions
- analytics dashboard
- admin tooling
- external candidate sources

Outcome:
- more robust platform for real recruiting workflows

## Phase 4 — Revenue and scale
Duration: ongoing

Goals:
- improve conversion and scale hiring network

Tasks:
- sales motion for startups
- higher-volume candidate sourcing
- broader role coverage
- new city expansion
- premium enterprise features

Outcome:
- sustainable growth and repeatable recruiting engine

## Suggested launch strategy
- launch with 10–20 startup customers
- focus on one or two role families first
- keep manual review and human ops in the loop
- use success-fee pricing early
- iterate fast on match quality before scaling

## Team plan
### Minimum team for v1
- 1 product lead
- 1 backend engineer
- 1 frontend engineer
- 1 AI/ML or data engineer
- 1 designer
- 1 QA / growth engineer
- 1 operations / recruiter partner

### Optional early hires
- founding talent operator
- customer success / partnerships lead
- data analyst

## KPI targets for MVP
- candidate activation rate > 25%
- time from role creation to shortlist < 24 hours
- intro acceptance rate > 30%
- recruiter time saved > 50%
- repeat company usage > 60%

## Go/no-go check
Proceed to scale only when:
- intro quality is strong
- candidate trust is acceptable
- companies are seeing good-fit candidates
- pricing and revenue loop work
- hiring process is materially faster than traditional sourcing
